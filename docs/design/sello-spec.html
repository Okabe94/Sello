Sello

The design specification for FinanceTracker: how every screen looks, behaves and moves, and the components it is built from. Where this document and the app disagree, this document is right and the app has a bug.

Version1.1, 8 October 2026
Default inkCobalto; Violeta in settings
PlatformAndroid, Jetpack Compose, Material 3
LanguageSpanish (es-CO), amounts in COP

1Principles

Sello is the paper trail of your money. Figures are printed on slips of paper. A verdict is one rubber stamp. Categories are circles that fill with ink. Five rules follow from that, and they settle most questions this document does not answer.

RuleWhat it means in practice
Figures live on paperEvery amount sits on a white slip. Colour is for the desk, the band and the ink, never behind a number.
One stamp per screenThe stamp is the verdict: Al día, Recibido, Lograda. A screen has at most one large stamp, so it is the first thing read after the total. There are no warning cards.
The answer comes firstEach slip opens with its total or its answer in large type. What explains it comes under a tear line. Home answers one question and stops.
Show it, do not write itIcons, size and position before sentences. A chart is at least 130dp tall and is a column, a bar or a line. One reference line at most, carrying its own label.
Adding is one motionType the amount, press Anotar. Everything else is already chosen and can be changed on the same screen.

Decisions behind this version

  • Direction chosen by the owner: Sello, with categories shown as filling circles, a bolder cobalt and slightly rounder corners.
  • Violet as an accent on cobalt was tried and dropped. Violet is a second ink the user can choose for the whole app.
  • Category detail replaced “Dónde compras” with “En números”.
  • Three tabs at most; settings is behind the gear; no pace graph on home; nothing under 12sp.

How money is written

  • Tabular figures everywhere (fontFeatureSettings = "tnum").
  • Thousands with a point, es-CO. No decimals for COP. Other currencies show two decimals with a comma: USD 2.340,00.
  • Hero and total amounts carry a peso sign at 56 % size, raised to cap height, at 72 % opacity. Lines inside a slip drop the sign, as a receipt does; the total line brings it back.
  • A gain or an income carries a plus. A loss or an overspend carries a true minus (U+2212), never a hyphen.
  • Abbreviations are for chart labels only: 3,53 M, 249 mil. A list or a slip always shows the full figure.
  • An amount that changes animates by counting (see Motion › Count).

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 500, filled. 18dp at the start of a line, 20 to 22dp in bars and rows, half the circle's diameter inside a category circle. Icons are drawn in brand; unselected tabs use the outlined style in inkSoft. Never put an icon in a coloured square.

4Structure and global behaviour

Recibo→Detalle de categoría→Editar categoría · Lista de gastos
Resumen→Ingresos · Lo que se repite · Detalle de categoría
Patrimonio→Detalle de inversión · Detalle de meta→Editores · Movimiento · Aportar
Dock (en todas)→Anotar→Anotar varios · Se repite · Ingreso
Recibo › engranaje→Configuración→Respaldo · Lo que se repite
  • Three tabs, each with its own back stack that is kept when switching tabs. Back from a tab root goes to Recibo; Back on Recibo leaves the app.
  • Route changes. HomeRoute stays the start destination. MetricsRoute becomes the second tab. InvestmentListRoute and GoalListRoute become the two halves of the third tab. SettingsRoute leaves the bar. ExpenseFormRoute, IncomeFormRoute, AssistantExpenseRoute and InvestmentEntryFormRoute all render the add sheet. BatchExpenseRoute and BatchInvestmentEntryRoute render Anotar varios.
  • Transitions. Push: the new screen slides in 30 % from the right and fades (motion.standard); pop is the reverse. Sheets rise. Tab switches cross-fade only (120 ms). The add sheet from the dock is a shared-element transition.
  • The selected month is shared by Recibo, Resumen and category detail, and resets to the current month when the app has been in the background for more than 30 minutes.
  • Process death. Anything typed in a sheet or a form (amount, category, lines of a bulk receipt) is kept in SavedStateHandle.
  • Deep links. financetracker://add-expense opens Anotar over Recibo. The widget’s button does the same.
  • Edge to edge. Content draws behind the system bars; the dock and tab bar add the gesture inset to their height.
  • Keyboard. When the system keyboard is open the dock and tab bar are hidden and the focused field scrolls into view.
  • Font scale. Layouts are checked at 1.0, 1.3 and 2.0. From 1.3: the category grid is one column, rows of three figures wrap to two lines, chips wrap. The hero amount shrinks before it clips.
  • Large screens and landscape have their own layouts, drawn below.
  • Language. Spanish only. Every string lives in strings.xml.

5Screens

Each screen is shown in light and dark with the Cobalto ink, using one month of sample data (October 2026, day 22 of 31). Mockups marked “scroll completo” show the whole scrolling content, taller than a phone.

1

Recibohome, tab 1

Answers “how am I doing and where is it going?” in one look. It is the start destination.

  • Blotter, tall. The title is the month.
  • Slip 1: what is left, the stamp, days left and amount per day.
  • Slip 2: every category as a filling circle, two columns.
  • Dock and tabs.
Content
  • Te quedan = sum of limits − spent this month. With no limits set, the label becomes “Has gastado” and the stamp is “Sin límite”.
  • Para N días = days left in the month including today. On the last day: “Para hoy”.
  • Puedes gastar … al día = (left − recurring charges still due this month) ÷ days left. If the result is negative it shows “$ 0 al día” in loss.
  • The stamp follows the table in 3 › Stamps.
  • Categories are ordered by the setting “Orden de las categorías” (default: most used first). Categories with no limit come last and show what was spent.
  • A past month shows “Te sobraron” or “Te pasaste por”, hides the per-day figure, and the stamp is “Bajo el límite” or “Pasado”.
Interactions
  • Tap a category → Detalle de categoría for the selected month.
  • Long-press a category → a small menu: Anotar aquí, Editar, Cambiar límite.
  • Tap the month → month sheet. Swipe the content sideways → previous or next month (not past the current one).
  • Tap the gear → Configuración.
  • Tap the dock → Anotar. Long-press the dock → Anotar varios.
  • Pull down has no action: there is nothing to refresh in an offline app.
States
  • Loading: two SlipSkeletons.
  • No categories: the first-run slip (below).
  • Categories but no expenses: the normal layout with empty circles and “Al día”.
  • More than ten categories: the grid simply continues; the slip grows and the screen scrolls.
Motion
  • On first composition the total counts up from 0 (motion.count) and the circles fill in order (motion.fill, 30 ms apart).
  • Returning from Anotar with a new expense: the total counts down, that category’s circle rises, nothing else moves.
  • Changing month: the slips cross-fade and slide 24dp in the direction of travel (motion.standard).
  • The stamp lands (motion.stamp) only when its text changed since the last time this month was shown.
Accessibility
  • Reading order: month, “Te quedan 937.200 pesos. Al día.”, days, per day, then each category cell, then the dock, then tabs.
  • The category grid is a list of 10 items for TalkBack (“1 de 10”).

First run

  • Shown while there are no categories.
  • “Crear categorías” opens the category editor; after the first one is saved the user returns here and step 1 is ticked.
  • “Traer mis datos de un respaldo” opens Respaldo.
  • The dock is visible but opens the category editor with a message until one category exists.
2

Anotaradd an expense or an income

The most used flow in the app. Everything needed to save is on one screen and the only required action is typing the amount.

  • The amount prints on the slip as a new line.
  • Under the tear line: what the month and the category will look like after saving.
  • All categories as chips; the last used one is selected.
  • Date, note, repeat, keypad, one button.
Opening
  • From the dock: a full-screen sheet that rises over the current screen; the dock morphs into the slip.
  • From a category (detail or long-press): the same, with that category selected.
  • From the deep link financetracker://add-expense?categoryName=…&amountCop=…: the same, pre-filled; an unknown category name selects nothing and highlights the chips.
  • Editing an existing expense opens the same screen titled “Editar gasto”, with a delete action in the top bar.
Fields
  • Amount (required): keypad only. Empty shows “$ 0” in inkSoft. Maximum 12 digits.
  • Category (required): one chip. All categories are shown, wrapping to as many rows as needed; past three rows the chip area scrolls on its own.
  • Date: defaults to today. Opens the date picker; future dates are allowed only when “Se repite” is on.
  • Note: optional free text, one line, 60 characters.
  • Se repite: off by default. Turning it on opens the sheet below.
Saving
  • The button is disabled until there is an amount and a category; the caption above it says what is missing.
  • “Anotar gasto” saves, plays the print feed, closes the sheet, and the origin screen updates (see Recibo › Motion).
  • A snackbar “Gasto anotado” offers “Deshacer” for 6 s.
  • If saving fails the sheet stays open with an ErrorSlip line above the button: “No se pudo guardar. Intenta de nuevo.”
  • Back or the close button with something typed asks “¿Descartar este gasto?” in a ConfirmSlip (not destructive colours).
The preview lines
  • “Te quedarán” = the month’s remaining amount after this expense; it counts as the user types.
  • “En [categoría]” = that category’s remaining amount after this expense; red with a minus if it goes over.
  • When the expense would take the category over its limit, the second line reads “Te pasas por 12.300 en Café”. Nothing blocks saving.
Top bar
  • Close, “Anotar”, a “varios” icon (opens Anotar varios keeping what was typed as the first line), and the Gasto / Ingreso switch.
  • Switching to Ingreso keeps the amount, date and note and swaps the chips and preview lines.
Accessibility
  • The amount is a live region: each key press announces the new amount.
  • Chips are a single-choice group. The save button announces the full sentence: “Anotar gasto de 48.700 pesos en Alimentación”.

Se repite

  • Opens when the switch is turned on, or when its row is tapped while on.
  • Frequency: Cada semana, Cada 15 días, Cada mes, Otro (every N days, weeks or months).
  • “El día” follows the frequency: a weekday, or a day of the month.
  • “Termina”: Nunca, or a date.
  • Closing the sheet without “Listo” turns the switch back off.
  • Saving creates the template and the first expense. Later ones are created by the daily worker and carry a repeat icon in lists.

Ingreso

  • The same sheet in income mode. The amount is green with a plus.
  • Chips are the income sources: Salario, Freelance, Ingresos pasivos, Transferencia, Otro. “Otro” reveals a field for the source name.
  • Preview: what the month’s income will add up to, and how much of it has no destination yet.
  • “Anotar ingreso” leads to the receipt in screen 8.
3

Anotar variosbulk add

For emptying your pockets at the end of the day: several expenses in one go, on one receipt.

  • One receipt with a line per expense and a running total.
  • The highlighted line is the one being edited: the keypad types its amount, the chips set its category, the field under them its note.
  • “Otro renglón” adds a line and moves the highlight to it.
  • One date for the whole receipt, in the top bar.
Behaviour
  • Opens from the “varios” icon in Anotar, from a long press on the dock, or from a category (pre-selecting it for every new line).
  • Starts with one empty line. A new line copies the category of the line above.
  • The receipt takes all the height between the top bar and the chips, so there is no empty band: the total sits at the bottom of the slip and the lines fill down toward it.
  • Tap a line to edit it. Swipe a line left to remove it (with undo). The last remaining line cannot be removed.
  • Lines with no amount are ignored when saving. A line with an amount but no category blocks saving and is outlined in loss.
  • The button counts the valid lines: “Anotar 3 gastos”. With one valid line it reads “Anotar gasto”.
Limits and layout
  • Up to 30 lines. The receipt scrolls inside its slip once it passes five lines; the total stays pinned at the bottom of the slip.
  • The category chips are a single scrolling row here, most used first.
  • Date applies to all lines. There is no repeat option in bulk.
After saving
  • The receipt feeds upward and off screen (motion.emphasized), the sheet closes, and the origin screen updates once, with every affected circle rising together.
  • Snackbar: “3 gastos anotados” with “Deshacer”, which removes all of them.
Investment movements
  • “Varios” in the movement sheet (screen 12) uses this same layout. Lines carry a movement type in place of a category, and each line has its own date.

Step by step

  • 1. Opening. One empty line, already highlighted, with the last used category. The keypad is live, so the first key press starts the first amount.
  • The save button and “Otro renglón” are disabled until the line has an amount.
  • If the user came from Anotar with something typed, that expense is line one and a second empty line is highlighted.
The loop. Type the amount → (optional) tap a category chip → (optional) tap the note field → tap “Otro renglón” or press Enter on a hardware keyboard. The new line copies the category above it, so a run of expenses in one category needs only amounts.
  • 2. Fixing and removing. A line with an amount and no category is outlined in red and says what is missing; the caption at the top of the receipt repeats it. Tapping the line highlights it so the chips apply to it.
  • Swiping a line left uncovers a red delete area; letting go past 40 % removes the line, with “Deshacer” in a snackbar. A long press offers the same in a menu.
  • The save button stays disabled, with the reason above the receipt, until every line with an amount has a category.

Varios movimientos

  • The same receipt for one investment. Each line has a movement type and its own date; tapping the date on a line opens the date picker.
  • There is no total, because contributions, dividends and valuations do not add up.
  • A “Valor actual” line may appear only once per date; a second one for the same day replaces the first after a confirmation.
  • Opened from “Varios” in the movement sheet, and from the investment’s overflow menu (“Anotar varios movimientos”), which is how a year of history is entered at once.
Rules shared by both
  • Lines are kept in SavedStateHandle; leaving with Back asks “¿Descartar 3 renglones?”.
  • All lines are written in one database transaction: either all are saved or none.
  • The highlighted line is always scrolled into view above the chips.
  • TalkBack reads each line as “Renglón 2 de 3. Farmacia Pasteur, 75.100 pesos, falta categoría”, with actions Editar and Borrar.
Validation messages
  • No amount on any line: “Escribe al menos un monto”.
  • Missing category: “A un renglón le falta categoría”.
  • More than 30 lines: “Otro renglón” is disabled with “Máximo 30 por recibo”.
  • Save failed: the receipt stays, with “No se pudo guardar. No se anotó ninguno.”

7Words and formats

  • Voice. Informal “tú”, short, concrete. “Te quedan”, “Puedes gastar”, “Anotar”. No exclamation marks: the stamp does the celebrating.
  • The verb is “anotar” for recording anything, everywhere: the dock, the sheet title, the button, the snackbar (“Gasto anotado”). “Aportar” for goals. “Guardar cambios” when editing. “Borrar” for deleting, never “Eliminar”.
  • Sentences in the interface say a fact with its number, not advice: “Cierras en $ 3.675.205”, not “¡Vas muy bien!”.
  • Errors say what happened and what to do: “No se pudo guardar. Intenta de nuevo.” They never apologise and never show a code.
  • Dates. In lists: “jue 22 oct”. Today and yesterday: “Hoy, jue 22”, “Ayer, mié 21”. Months alone: “oct”, “feb 27” when the year differs. Full: “22 de octubre de 2026”.
  • Numbers in charts may abbreviate (3,53 M; 249 mil). Per cents use a comma and a thin space: “+4,6 %”.
  • Capitals only where Spanish wants them and inside stamps.

8Accessibility

  • Every text pair meets 4.5:1 and every mark 3:1 in all four colour schemes (section 2).
  • State is never colour alone: a category over its limit has a full circle, a minus sign and red; a loss has a minus and the word “pierde”.
  • All touch targets are at least 48dp, including chips (36dp tall with a 48dp hit box).
  • TalkBack: slips are headings when they have a title; figures are read with their currency; charts have a text summary; stamps are read as plain words.
  • Nothing depends on a gesture alone: swipe-to-delete has a menu equivalent on long press; swiping months has the month sheet; the dock’s long press has the “varios” icon in the sheet.
  • Font scale to 2.0 without clipping (see Navigation › Font scale).
  • Animations respect the system setting; no content is conveyed only by motion.
  • The keypad, chips and switches work with a hardware keyboard and Switch Access; focus order follows reading order.
